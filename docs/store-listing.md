## Full description

The planet is stranger than you think.

OddAtlas is a hand-picked catalogue of the oddest places on Earth that you can see from above — a 40 km
bullseye in the Sahara, a crater that has been on fire for decades, a heart-shaped island, a pink lake,
a scale model of a mountain range hidden in a desert compound, and many more.

• Every place checked against real satellite imagery
• Tap a marker to fly there in satellite view
• Search and filter by category and tags; sort by name, country or distance
• "Random strange place" button for instant discoveries
• Add your own finds — paste a Google Maps link and the coordinates fill in
• Export and import your atlas as JSON
• Light and dark themes · no account · no ads · no tracking
