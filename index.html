About OddAtlas

OddAtlas is a hand-curated catalogue of strange places on Earth that you can see from above. Every seed entry was checked against satellite imagery.

Add your own finds — they're saved on this device. Export your catalogue as JSON to back it up or share it, or propose a place for everyone by opening a pull request that edits data/places.json on GitHub.
