Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

  • OddAtlas has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Places you add, edit or delete are stored only on your device (browser local storage). Exports are files you choose where to save.
  • Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap and Esri (World Imagery); those providers receive the standard request data (IP address, requested map area) under their own policies.
  • "Open in Google Maps" links take you to Google, under Google's policies.
